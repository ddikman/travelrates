TravelRates is a fast, simple currency converter app built for backpackers and independent travelers. Compare several currencies side by side, then keep converting with saved rates when you’re offline.

Built for the road:
• Compare multiple currencies at once
• Use saved exchange rates without internet
• Refresh rates automatically when online
• Search by currency, code, or country
• Split or multiply costs with the built-in calculator
• Switch between light and dark modes
• No ads, subscriptions, or in-app purchases

From airport SIM cards and exchange desks to night markets, hostels, tours, and shared costs, TravelRates helps you understand prices quickly without accounts, graphs, or clutter.

Rates update from the internet once a day and are stored on your device for offline use. Exchange rates are for reference and may differ from rates offered by your bank, card provider, or exchange service.
