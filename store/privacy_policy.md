# TravelRates Privacy Policy

Effective: 11 September 2026

TravelRates is designed to work without an account and to keep your travel data on your device.

## Information stored on your device

Your selected currencies, entered amounts, saved exchange rates, display settings, and review-prompt state are stored locally on your device. TravelRates does not upload this information to us. Deleting the app removes this locally stored information, subject to your device backup settings.

## Exchange-rate service

When TravelRates refreshes exchange rates, it contacts `currencyrates.greycastle.se`. The request is used only to return current reference rates. This service does not retain IP addresses or access logs.

## Crash reporting

Production versions use Firebase Crashlytics to help us diagnose unexpected errors and improve app stability. Crashlytics may receive crash stack traces and crash state, device and operating-system metadata, app version, session metadata, and an installation identifier. We do not attach account details, user IDs, entered currency amounts, selected currencies, custom behavioral logs, or expected offline and network errors to crash reports.

Crash information is collected only for app stability. It is not used for advertising or tracking and is not sold or shared by us for third-party advertising. Data is encrypted in transit. Firebase states that Crashlytics crash reports and associated identifiers are retained for 90 days.

Crashlytics is disabled in debug and test builds.

## Analytics, advertising, and accounts

TravelRates does not use usage analytics, advertising SDKs, personalized advertising, or cross-app tracking. It does not require or provide user accounts.

## Contact

For privacy questions, use the support contact shown on the TravelRates store listing.
